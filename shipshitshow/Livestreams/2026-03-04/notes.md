It is not just engineers getting cut. The real massacre is middle management. And nobody is crying about it.

What does a middle manager actually do? Route information up and down. Translate strategy into tasks. Run standups. Write status updates. Track progress.

AI does every single one of those things now. Better. Faster. Without the politics.

- AI agents can break down strategy into tasks automatically
- AI can track progress, flag blockers, write updates
- AI does not schedule unnecessary meetings to justify its existence
- The org chart is collapsing: CEO > AI layer > builders. That is it.
CLIP 6: Middle management is dead and nobody misses it - the most relatable clip for anyone who has sat through a pointless standup. This one could go viral on its own.
