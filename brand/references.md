# Reference study

Inspected **15 October 2026**: [Silicon Carne channel](https://www.youtube.com/@siliconcarnepod), current thumbnails and [a recent on-air episode](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CTQVl7t70nE).

Observed: compact avatar, full wordmark/host portrait in banner, faces/short headlines/stable signature in thumbnails, small on-air mark/topic label, and natural-color cameras. Thumbnail imagery can be busy; consistency and hierarchy are the useful principles. No giant persistent company footer appeared in the sampled discussion; that does not establish that sponsors never appear elsewhere.

Carlos Diaz [announced a complete rebrand on 1 September 2026](https://siliconcarne.substack.com/p/silicon-carne-cest-maintenant-tous), crediting Studio Smoooth. Older DYLM/Wizard Studio portfolios describe earlier work.

Use these roles and consistency, not their SC/slash mark, portraits, textures or motion. This analysis does not establish causal effects on CTR, views, retention or revenue. No reference screenshots or third-party assets are bundled here.
