Fable 5 is gone. GPT-5.6 is preview-gated. Frontier models are still incredibly useful, but access is now part of the architecture.

Tonight we’re asking the practical question:

What replaces Fable 5 when you still need serious AI coding help?

Not in theory. In the actual workflow.

We’ll look at open model alternatives like GLM, Kimi, DeepSeek, Qwen, Mistral, Llama, and GPT-OSS, then map where they actually fit:

What should run locally?
What should run hosted?
What belongs in Bedrock, Ollama, LM Studio, or vLLM?
What still needs Claude, GPT, or another frontier model?
And what breaks when your best model disappears?

The point is not “open models beat closed models.”

The point is survivability.

Closed models optimize for peak capability.
Open models give you fallback paths, cost control, and infrastructure you can keep calling.

If your entire AI coding workflow depends on one gated model being available tomorrow, that is not an AI stack.

That is a single point of failure.
