# AI Won't Kill Open Source. It Will Save It

## Summary

Over 50% of GitHub pull requests are now AI-generated. Maintainers are drowning. TLDraw shut down all external contributions. The T3 codebase got 150 PRs in 5 days. People are submitting junk just to pad their resumes.

Theo dropped a video saying open source is dying. 
We react to his key points, agree where he's right, and explain why his conclusion is wrong.

The answer isn't less AI. It's better AI. Automated triage, trust systems like Vouch, and AI reviewing AI code before a human ever has to look at it. The maintainers who adopt AI tools will scale. The ones who don't will drown.
