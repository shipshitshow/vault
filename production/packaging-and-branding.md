# Packaging, branding and broadcast assets

Use the actual source board for an upcoming live, the full transcript for a completed stream, and the final edit for a recap/Short. Model names, prices, outcomes and chapters must be supported by that asset. Title, description, thumbnail and opening should promise the same useful answer.

## Metadata and tests

Provide distinct title candidates and a focused tag list (`youtube_tags` in vault). Description: human opener, practical takeaway, accurate chapters, primary/source/example links and channel footer. Chapters use the asset's own clock. Tags mainly help spelling/ambiguity; keyword stuffing is not a growth strategy.

Create exactly three thumbnail hypotheses. For a thumbnail-only test, hold title fixed. For combined tests, label title/thumbnail pairs. A completed eligible livestream archive can be tested; scheduled lives and Shorts cannot. YouTube's test evaluates watch time and may be inconclusive. Do not call a prompt a proven winner. [Current test guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en)

## Channel identity

Exact name: **Ship Sh!t Show**. Latest direction: photographic indie rock'n'roll, red/black/warm ivory, grain and gig-poster energy, real hosts at computers, build/check/ship/improve and happy users. Use original host references and preserve likeness. Current user corrections override a default palette/layout. Avoid reviving rejected illustrated/cube concepts.

PFP and banner have different jobs: tiny circular recognition versus responsive channel promise. Export separate masters and final assets; inspect circular and mobile/desktop/TV crops. Recheck [YouTube specifications](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10456525?hl=en) before export.

## Restream

Plan discussion, screen-demo, partner and closing scenes. Use native layout/name controls. Generate only required backgrounds, transparent overlays, logos or motion assets. Leave captions/nameplates clear; keep product content readable. Test the actual Studio windows rather than guessing their positions.

Video uploaded as a graphic does not play audio. An audible bump needs the appropriate clip/media source. Recheck dimensions, file limits and plan eligibility in [Restream graphics guidance](https://support.restream.io/en/articles/8540835-add-graphics-to-your-stream-in-studio). Existing `_labs` previews are drafts, not the completed broadcast package.

## Partners

Obtain offer/CTA, audience, relationship, approved evidence and actual host experience. Draft a natural disclosed host read: viewer problem, supported benefit/example, one CTA, return to episode. Never invent personal endorsement or guaranteed results. Mark claims awaiting confirmation. Recheck [YouTube branded-content policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/17596007?hl=en) and applicable market requirements before publication. Drafting does not authorize partner outreach or accepting terms.
